Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our microphones, interfaces, and studio gear.

Every bundle comes fully tested with studio microphones, compatible desktop or boom mounts, shock absorbing mounts, shielded low-noise audio cables, and pop filters.

Condenser XLR microphones require 48V phantom power, which is built into all our included audio interfaces and podcast mixer consoles. Dynamic mics like our Studio Pro operate without phantom power.

All our audio interfaces, USB microphones, and digital mixers feature class-compliant USB-C connectivity compatible with macOS, Windows, iPadOS, and popular DAWs like Logic, Audacity, and Reaper.

USB microphones plug directly into a computer for simple plug-and-play recording. XLR microphones output an analog balanced signal to an audio interface or mixer, offering superior preamps, lower noise floor, and hardware expandability.

Yes, all audio gear orders ship worldwide with insured courier delivery and include a minimum 2-year manufacturer warranty with dedicated audio engineering tech support.

Our multi-channel audio interfaces and broadcast consoles support standard XLR inputs, letting you easily expand your studio setup with additional microphones and monitoring gear at any time.

Studio tiers include four dynamic broadcast mics, four pro boom arms, four closed-back studio headphones, bi-amped active studio monitors, and high-purity Mogami XLR cables.

We offer a 30-day studio trial period on all hardware kits with full refund support if the equipment doesn't match your acoustic requirements.
