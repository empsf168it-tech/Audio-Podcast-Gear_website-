Our Story

From a boutique audio lab to a global hardware brand.

PODVOX began in an acoustic testing room with a single mission: to provide creators, broadcasters, and studio producers with audio equipment that delivers studio-grade warmth without prohibitive pricing.

Today, our microphones, audio interfaces, monitoring headphones, and boom arms power over 50,000 podcasts and broadcast studios worldwide. Every product is engineered with custom voice-tuned capsules, low-noise preamps, and durable all-metal construction.
